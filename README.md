## 📌 Objective

To understand the fundamentals of Adverse Drug Reactions (ADRs), their classification, and the importance of Pharmacovigilance in ensuring patient safety.

---

## 📖 What is an Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR)?

An Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) is a harmful, unintended, and unwanted response to a medicine that occurs at normal doses used for the prevention, diagnosis, or treatment of disease.

### Example
A patient develops a skin rash after taking Amoxicillin at the prescribed dose.

---

# 📚 Types of ADR

| Type | Name | Description | Example |
|------|------|-------------|---------|
| A | Augmented | Dose-related and predictable | Bleeding with Warfarin |
| B | Bizarre | Unpredictable and allergic | Penicillin Allergy |
| C | Chronic | Due to long-term therapy | Steroid-induced osteoporosis |
| D | Delayed | Appears after months or years | Drug-induced cancer |
| E | End of Use | Withdrawal reactions | Opioid withdrawal |
| F | Failure | Unexpected treatment failure | Antibiotic resistance |

---

# 🩺 What is Pharmacovigilance?

Pharmacovigilance is the science and activities related to:

- Detecting ADRs
- Assessing ADRs
- Understanding ADRs
- Preventing ADRs

Its primary goal is to improve patient safety and ensure medicines are used safely.

---

# 🔍 Steps for ADR Identification

1. Collect patient information
2. Review medical history
3. Record all medications
4. Identify suspected medicine
5. Check timing of symptoms
6. Assess causality
7. Document findings
8. Report the ADR

---

# 📝 How to Report an ADR

Healthcare professionals should:

✅ Record patient information

✅ Record suspected medicine

✅ Describe the adverse reaction

✅ Mention seriousness

✅ Submit the report to the Pharmacovigilance Programme of India (PvPI)

# 📋 Sample Case Study

### Patient

- Age: 45 years
- Gender: Female

### Medicine

Paracetamol 500 mg

### Symptoms

- Skin rash
- Itching
- Mild swelling

### Assessment

The reaction appeared shortly after taking Paracetamol.

### Suspected ADR

Drug-induced allergic skin reaction.

### Action Taken

Medicine stopped and physician informed.

---

# 🎯 Conclusion

Adverse Drug Reactions are an important aspect of patient safety.

Early detection, proper assessment, and timely reporting through Pharmacovigilance help improve medicine safety and protect public health.
